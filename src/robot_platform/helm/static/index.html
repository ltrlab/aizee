    JOINT LIMITS · MOTORS STAY DISABLED

    Sweep each joint by hand

    Move one joint at a time to each end of its travel and hold it there for a moment. An end counts once two consecutive readings agree, so a bump or a flick cannot widen a limit. Confirm each joint, then save.

    HEAD

    The head's servos stay limp: turn the head by hand. Read the head, hold it straight ahead and level and set its reference. Turn each joint and watch the 3D view's head: Flip a joint that turns the other way, then Looks right. Then sweep each joint to both ends, confirm both and save. Last, the shoulder corners: turn the head to a side and tilt it down until it is just clear of the shoulder, and record that corner; record each side's end and halfway, then save. Live sessions take the head only once its shoulder clearance is saved. Any step can be done again; a new reference asks for a new sweep and new corners.

                Manual motor tuning

                Edit, hold with T, apply, then engage with T to test. Editing alone does not change motors. Unchecked joints hold position; their motors stay powered. Blank travel uses the full usable range. A travel window stays centered on the first Enable pose; Shutdown returns to reference outside that window.

                Gripper zero

                Sets a gripper's motor zero where its jaws are and saves it on the drive. A gripper's zero is fully closed: inner jaw faces together. Use it after a gear skip, when the closed gripper no longer reads 0 rad. The arms stay disabled and the live view pauses while it runs; a power cycle confirms the drive kept the zero.

                Left gripper

                Close the left gripper's jaws fully by hand, inner faces together, and hold them still. Its zero is set at this pose and saved on the drive.

                Right gripper

                Close the right gripper's jaws fully by hand, inner faces together, and hold them still. Its zero is set at this pose and saved on the drive.

                Gripper force feedback

                While the robot's gripper holds something, the trigger meets a wall where the object stopped the jaws: no push at the wall, and a push that grows as you squeeze past it. It never pulls closed. Switch it on in Teleop. Every setting is saved for every session and applies at once to a running one.

                200 mA is the leader boards' limit, and strength scales the whole push. The push reaches the maximum this far past the wall: one degree of trigger squeezes the jaws about 2.5 Nm, so a held cube sits about 0.8° past it and a light hold 0.2-0.3°. Fewer degrees make a harder wall; if the trigger buzzes at the wall, add some.

                The wall appears once the gripper has stayed still, squeezing past the threshold, for the hold time, and goes once you let go. The gripper's own friction reads 0.30-0.35 Nm at rest, and a free close starts stuck for up to about 100 ms: a lower threshold or a shorter hold finds contact sooner but can put a brief wall in a free close.

                Start a session to see each gripper's squeeze here.

                  Measured feedback Stale / unavailable

                  Select a joint by clicking it on the model or in the joint picker. Click empty space, press Esc, close its card or pick “No joint selected” to clear it. Missing readings show “—”; stale readings never appear live. Outside a session the torque-off reader supplies position and drive temperature only.

                  On the model: each joint's dial shows its calibrated range (grey), reference (white tick), travel from the reference (blue) and position (needle), with one position label. The violet ring grows with torque as a share of the receiver's limit; the hub shades from slate to amber to red as temperature nears its limit. Dials need a verified direction mapping; stale readings turn grey. The selected joint keeps its card.
