Minerva head tracking

The robot's head turns with this headset while a teleop session follows (Engage, T). Without it the head holds still at its zero.

Connecting to Helm…

Look straight ahead and level when you start: that is the head's zero. To zero again, look where the head should face straight ahead and pull either trigger (or pinch); recentering the headset (hold the Meta button) zeroes at the new heading, level. Take the headset off or press the Meta button to stop: the head holds, then returns to its zero.
