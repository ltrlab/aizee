Minerva head tracking

The robot's head turns with this headset while a teleop session follows (Engage, T). Without it the head holds still at its zero.

Connecting to Helm…

Look straight ahead and level when you start: that is the head's zero. Inside you see the head camera's picture. Press A or X for the ring of controls, in front of you and a little low: tilt that controller's thumbstick toward an item and press A or X again to choose it; with the stick centred, A or X goes back, or closes the ring. From the ring you start a session (Virtual, an arm, both arms or the head), step it (Idle, Enable, Engage, Hold), shut it down, end it or Disable; anything that powers or moves Minerva asks you to hold A or X for a second. Starting here takes Helm's control: the other device's page shows the session, view-only, and can still Disable or Shut down. The ring's View sets the picture: Follow head, 3D (the head camera's depth, fixed in the room while a session drives the head), Model, Wrists (the wrist cameras' pictures on the model's wrists), Map, the opacities and Recenter; the headset remembers them. While the ring is open on a controller, its arm takes no part. With the ring closed, a line low in view shows a fault, a hold or lost control. To zero again, look where the head should face straight ahead and press either controller's thumbstick (a pinch does not: your hands hold the leaders); recentering the headset (hold the Meta button) zeroes at the new heading, level. Take the headset off or press the Meta button to stop: the head holds, then returns to its zero.
